Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

Note for operators: PostyFox is self-hosted software — there is no central "PostyFox" company processing your data. This policy describes what this instance, running the PostyFox platform, collects and does with it. Replace [Operator] and [contact address] below with your own details before publishing.

1. Who this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to this PostyFox instance, operated by [Operator] ("we", "us", "the operator"). It explains what information is collected when you use this instance to compose and deliver posts to third-party platforms (Discord, Telegram, Bluesky, Tumblr, FurAffinity, and Fediverse/ActivityPub servers such as Mastodon), and how that information is stored, used, and protected.

2. Information we collect

  • Account identity. Your username and email address, as provided by the sign-in provider (Keycloak) behind this instance's login page. We do not receive or store your password — authentication is handled entirely by the identity provider and a session cookie issued by the edge proxy.
  • Connector configuration and credentials. When you connect a platform (e.g. a Discord webhook, a Telegram account, a Bluesky app password, or a paired browser session for FurAffinity), we store the configuration and any tokens, session cookies, or credentials required to post on your behalf. These are held in the platform's secret store and are only ever sent back out to the corresponding third-party platform when delivering your posts.
  • Post content and media. Text, tags, and any images or files you compose or upload are stored so posts can be generated, delivered, and their status tracked, and so you can review your posting history.
  • Templates, tag presets, and triggers. Any reusable templates, tag presets, or external-trigger (webhook) configurations you create.
  • API keys. If you create an API key for programmatic access, we store a salted hash of the key — never the key itself — so it can be verified but not recovered.
  • Technical and diagnostic data. Basic request telemetry (timing and error traces via OpenTelemetry) generated by your browser session, used only to operate and troubleshoot this instance. This does not include the content of your posts.

3. How we use this information

Information is used solely to operate the service you're using, specifically to:

  • authenticate you and keep your session secure;
  • render and deliver your posts to the platforms you select;
  • show you the status and history of posts you've submitted;
  • let you manage connectors, templates, tag presets, triggers, and API keys; and
  • diagnose and fix technical problems with the service.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Sharing with third-party platforms

PostyFox's purpose is to deliver the content you compose to the platforms you connect. When you submit a post, its content and any attached media are sent directly to each selected platform (Discord, Telegram, Bluesky, Tumblr, FurAffinity, or a Fediverse server) using your connected credentials. Once delivered, that content is subject to the destination platform's own privacy policy and terms of service — we recommend reviewing those separately. We do not share your data with any other third party.

5. Where data is stored

Data is stored on infrastructure operated by [Operator]: structured data (accounts, connectors, posts, templates, triggers) in a PostgreSQL database, uploaded media in S3-compatible object storage, and secrets/credentials in a dedicated secrets store. Some connectors (e.g. Telegram) additionally retain a persistent session on the destination platform's own servers in order to post on your behalf.

6. Cookies

This instance uses a single session cookie set by the login proxy (oauth2-proxy) to keep you signed in. It contains no tracking identifiers and is not used by any third-party advertising or analytics service.

7. Data retention and deletion

Posts, templates, tag presets, triggers, connectors, and API keys are retained until you delete them yourself from the relevant page, or until you ask us to remove them. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact [contact address]. Removing a connector deletes its stored credentials; it does not retract content already delivered to the destination platform.

8. Security

Access to this instance requires authentication, and API keys are stored only as salted hashes. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored data, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure. If you believe your credentials have been compromised, disconnect the affected connector and rotate any exposed credentials immediately.

9. Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required to hold an account on the platforms it posts to.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data should be directed to [contact address].
